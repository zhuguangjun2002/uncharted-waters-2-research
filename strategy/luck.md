# Luck

Advice for the strategy pages. The wiki's Church and Mosque page gives the
donation rule, and Duties and the House of Fortune page what mates' Luck does
and how to read it; how to use them belongs here.

## Donate everything while carrying little

A donation's Luck depends on what share of the gold you carry it is, not on
its size: giving all you carry adds the full 10. So the cheapest way to raise
Luck is to give everything while carrying little, just above the random
minimum a gift must reach (at most 500 gold): donating all of 500 gold gives
the full +10. Bank the rest first.

Rule: `game-details/buildings.md`, Donate (`0x32AF0`); the wiki's Church and
Mosque page.

## Check a mate's Luck before giving them a job

A mate's Luck never changes: praying, donating, level-ups, and every sea event
touch only yours. It matters in only a few places: the best Luck among you,
your First Mate, and your Chief Navigator guards against storms and other
anomalies, and each captain's Luck guards their ship in the two Missing Ship
regions. So a lucky mate is worth one of those jobs, and an unlucky one adds
nothing there. The House of Fortune's Mates reading tells you which, once and
for all, for 50 gold: “Your mate is one lucky sea dog!” means 75–99, and
“What good fortune...” means 100.

Rule: `game-details/stats.md`, How attributes are raised and lowered;
`game-details/at-sea.md`, Checks every four hours and Missing Ship
(`0x1ECF8`, `0x1EAD2`); `game-details/buildings.md`, House of Fortune.
